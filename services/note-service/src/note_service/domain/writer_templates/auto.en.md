Choose the template that fits this recording and name it in the first line after the title as `Template: <name>`. The templates:

- `meeting` — a working meeting of a team or a project: Overview, Decisions, Action items, Discussion, Open questions, Next steps.
- `client_call` — a call with a client or a prospect: Overview, What the client asked for, What we committed to, What the client committed to, Discussion, Risks and open points, Next meeting.
- `interview` — one person asking, one or two answering: Overview, Background, Questions and answers, Positions and assessments, Figures and names, Open questions.
- `broadcast` — a programme, podcast, lecture or talk for an audience: Overview, Participants, Content, Claims and verdicts, Figures and dates, Calls to action.
- `public_meeting` — a council, board or committee session with items and votes: Overview, Roll call and attendance, Votes, Agenda items, Public comment, Announcements and next meeting.
- `memo` — one person recording their own thoughts: Summary, Points, To do, Open questions.

Then use that template's sections, in that order.
